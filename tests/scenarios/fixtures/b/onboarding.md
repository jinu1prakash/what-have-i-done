# New Starter Onboarding

## Before day one
Order a laptop from IT. Ask your manager for the team handbook.
Accounts are created by IT once HR marks you as started.

## Day one
Meet your manager at 9:30. Collect your badge from reception (ground floor).
Your buddy will walk you through the codebase.

## Week one
Complete the security training. It is mandatory and must be done in week one.
Set up your dev environment - see the handbook.
Book a 1:1 with your skip-level.
